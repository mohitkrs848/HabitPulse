# HabitPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

HabitPulse is an offline-first habit tracker. This policy describes the current application implementation. Update the effective date and publish this text at a stable public URL before submitting to an app store.

## Information We Store

HabitPulse stores habit names, descriptions, categories, colors, icons, schedules, completion dates, daily notes, reminder times, and appearance preferences in local application storage on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send this information to a HabitPulse server.

## Collection And Analytics

The current application does not implement advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, account registration, or a cloud synchronization service. No habit records are transmitted by the app as part of normal use.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, HabitPulse requests operating-system notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. Notifications are not sent from a server. You can change permissions in Android settings and remove reminders in the app.

## Backups And Sharing

When you export a JSON or CSV backup, HabitPulse creates a local file and opens the operating system share sheet. The destination you choose is governed by that destination's privacy practices. HabitPulse does not receive a copy. Importing a JSON backup replaces local app data after confirmation.

## Retention And Deletion

Your records remain in app storage until you delete them, clear HabitPulse storage, or uninstall the app. Archived records remain on-device until deleted. Exported files are managed by the location or application where you saved or shared them.

## Children's Privacy

HabitPulse does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The application has no account or online profile.

## Changes And Contact

This policy may be updated when application behavior changes. The Play Store listing should link to the latest published version. Add a publisher contact email before release; no contact address is provided in this source repository.
