## Full Description

Make room for the habits that matter.

HabitPulse helps you build consistency with a calm daily dashboard, flexible schedules, useful streaks, and clear progress. Create a routine that fits your real life, then check in one day at a time.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Create, edit, duplicate, archive, restore, and organize habits.
• Choose daily, weekly target, selected weekdays, or every-N-days schedules.
• Mark a habit complete, undo a check-in, and keep a dated history.
• Track current and longest streaks, total completions, and success rates.
• Add a private note to any day of a habit.
• Star favorites and search by habit name or category.
• Set multiple local reminder times for a habit.
• Review daily, weekly, and monthly progress with charts and a completion calendar.
• Work toward milestones for starting habits and keeping a streak.
• Export a JSON backup or CSV completion history, and restore a JSON backup.
• Choose light, dark, or system appearance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No login. No signup. No ads. No analytics. No cloud sync. Your habits and settings are stored on your device. Reminders are scheduled locally. Backups are shared only when you choose an export destination.

HabitPulse is intentionally focused: simple tools for steady progress, with your data under your control.
